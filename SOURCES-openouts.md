# Sources for "Open it out with" material

Every factual claim in `openouts.js` was checked against a source before it was
written. This file records what was checked and where, so any figure in the
material can be traced. Entries are listed by theme and sub-theme.

A claim that could not be confirmed was left out rather than softened. Two
examples from Theme 1: the exact date of India's last smallpox case is reported
inconsistently across sources, so the material says "May 1975"; and the FRBM
Act's deficit target was dropped from the preparedness entry because the
searched sources did not tie it to disaster financing.

---

## Theme 1 — Ends, Means and the Ethics of Decision

### 1.1 Do the means contaminate the end?

**No-first-use and the threat never carried out**
Doctrine adopted by the Cabinet Committee on Security, January 2003; credible
minimum deterrence; retaliation only against a nuclear attack on Indian
territory or forces; retaliation "massive" and designed to inflict unacceptable
damage; reserved nuclear response to major chemical or biological attack.
- Arms Control Association, *Twenty-Five Years of Overt Nuclear India*
- Observer Research Foundation, *Beyond Current Nuclear Doctrine Debates*

**Force as signal rather than as objective**
Uri attack 18 September 2016, 19 soldiers killed; strikes on launch pads across
the LoC 29 September 2016. Pulwama bombing 14 February 2019, 40 CRPF personnel
killed; Balakot strike 26 February 2019; Foreign Secretary's description of it
as a non-military pre-emptive action.
- *2019 Balakot airstrike*, Wikipedia
- Stimson Center, *Three Years After Balakot*
- The Round Table, "India's Pakistan policy: from 2016 'surgical strike' to 2019 Balakot 'airstrike'"

**Coercion without combat**
UN Security Council Resolution 661, August 1990, comprehensive sanctions on
Iraq; oil-for-food programme created December 1996; criticism of delays,
reparation deductions and politically motivated holds; later shift to targeted
sanctions.
- Congressional Research Service, *Iraq: Humanitarian Needs, Impact of Sanctions, and the "Oil for Food" Program*
- MERIP, *The Enduring Lessons of the Iraq Sanctions*
- Global Policy Forum, *Iraq Sanctions: Humanitarian Implications and Options for the Future*

**A lawful end pursued unlawfully**
PUCL v State of Maharashtra (2014) 10 SCC 635: 99 encounters, 135 killed by
Mumbai police 1995–1997; sixteen guidelines; force of law under Article 141;
independent investigation required. National Security Act 1980: three months
before Advisory Board reference, Board of three High Court judges, twelve month
maximum, no legal representation before the Board.
- *PUCL v State of Maharashtra*, Indian Kanoon
- Bar and Bench, "Even State has no authority to violate Article 21"
- Ministry of Home Affairs, text of the National Security Act 1980

**When the outcome may genuinely outweigh the route**
Bihar and Uttar Pradesh about three-quarters of global smallpox cases in 1974;
Operation Smallpox Zero 1975; documented intimidation and coercion in the final
stages; last indigenous Indian case May 1975; global eradication declared 1980.
- Greenough, P., "Intimidation, coercion and resistance in the final stages of the South Asian Smallpox Eradication Campaign, 1973–1975", PubMed
- *1974 smallpox epidemic in India*, Wikipedia
- University of Michigan online exhibit, *Zero Smallpox*

### 1.3 Intuition, evidence and the limits of calculation

**What a trial establishes and what it cannot**
2019 Nobel to Banerjee, Duflo and Kremer; Kremer's Kenya schooling trials from
the mid-1990s; internal validity from randomisation; Deaton and Cartwright on
external validity.
- Nobel Prize press release, Economic Sciences 2019
- Deaton and Cartwright, NBER Working Paper 22595
- CEPR, *What randomisation can and cannot do*

**When the measure becomes the target**
Goodhart's law; Campbell's law; SDG India Index across 113 indicators aligned to
the National Indicator Framework; Aspirational Districts Programme launched
January 2018 covering 112 districts with delta rankings.
- *Goodhart's law*, Wikipedia
- NITI Aayog, SDG India Index 2023-24 (PIB release)

**A model that travels, and what it needs to survive the journey**
MGR launched the Tamil Nadu noon meal scheme 1 July 1982; national programme
1995; Supreme Court order of 28 November 2001 requiring 300 calories and 8–12g
protein for 200 days. Gandaman, Saran district, 16 July 2013: 23 children died,
monocrotophos, oil taken from a pesticide container.
- Ramanathapuram District administration, Puratchi Thalaivar MGR Noon Meal Programme
- *Bihar school meal poisoning incident*, Wikipedia
- Down To Earth, "A year on, no lessons learnt from Bihar mid day meal tragedy"

**When the default is denial**
Drèze and colleagues: about 1,000 households across 32 villages in Jharkhand,
exclusion up to twenty per cent where biometric authentication was required for
every sale. Right to Food Campaign: at least 57 hunger-related deaths 2015–2018,
at least 19 linked to Aadhaar-related exclusion.
- Drèze et al., *Aadhaar and Food Security in Jharkhand: Pain without Gain?*
- The Wire, "Aadhaar-Based PDS Means Denial of Rations For Many"

**What the field knows that the file does not**
Shift from mass vaccination to search and containment; ring vaccination around
each case; Bihar and UP as the centre of the endgame.
- University of Michigan online exhibit, *Chronology of the eradication campaign*
- Vaccine, "Lessons from smallpox eradication campaign in Bihar state and in India"

### 1.4 When custom is not a guide

- *Shakti Vahini v Union of India*, AIR 2018 SC 1601

## Theme 2 — Knowledge, Education and the Doubting Mind

**NEP 2020: structure changed, formation still open**
Union Cabinet approval 29 July 2020; 5+3+3+4 structure for ages 3–18 replacing
10+2; 50% gross enrolment ratio in higher education targeted by 2035.
- Ministry of Education, National Education Policy 2020
- MyGov, NEP 2020 portal

**Why rote is rational for the student**
NCRB: 13,999 student suicides in 2022 against 7,696 in 2011. Kota: 32 in 2023,
highest since 2015; more than two lakh aspirants a year. Rajasthan Coaching
Centres (Control and Regulation) Act 2025: registration, no enrolment below 16,
five study hours a day, penalties of Rs 2 lakh and Rs 5 lakh.
- National Crime Records Bureau, Accidental Deaths and Suicides in India
- PRS, The Rajasthan Coaching Centres (Control and Regulation) Bill 2025
- The Week, "Why student suicides in Kota are a distress call"

**Where the basics are missing, formation cannot start**
ASER 2024: 649,491 children, 17,997 villages, 605 districts. Class 5 reading a
Class 2 text 38.5% (2022) to 44.8% (2024); Class 3 in government schools 16.3%
to 23.4%, highest since 2005.
- ASER Centre, Annual Status of Education Report 2024
- Ideas for India, "ASER 2024: More than a post-pandemic recovery in learning"

**Employability as precondition rather than rival**
India Skills Report 2025 (CII with Wheebox and AICTE): overall graduate
employability 54.81% in 2025 against 51.25% in 2024; engineering about 71.5%,
management about 78%, science about 58%.
- India Skills Report 2025, CII / Wheebox / AICTE

**Where mastery genuinely requires retained content**
Argument, not claim. No sourced figures.

**ANRF and whether the structure rewards risk**
Act assented 15 August 2023, commenced 1 December 2023; Rs 50,000 crore for
2023–28; Rs 14,000 crore budgeted from the Centre, the remaining Rs 36,000 crore
(about 80%) expected from non-government sources.
- PIB, Parliament passes the Anusandhan National Research Foundation Bill 2023
- Office of the Principal Scientific Adviser, ANRF page
- PRS, The Anusandhan National Research Foundation Bill 2023

**When institutional doubt stops working**
Replication failures in psychology and cancer biology; predatory journals;
reviewer incentives. General literature, no single figure cited.

**Scientific temper as a constitutional duty**
Article 51A(h), inserted by the 42nd Amendment 1976.
- Constitution of India, Part IVA

**Where legitimate doubt ends**
Argument, not claim. No sourced figures.

**Recorded dissent against manufactured consensus**
RBI Monetary Policy Committee publishes minutes recording each member's vote.
- Reserve Bank of India, MPC minutes

**Intuition and proof in the Indian mathematical tradition**
Aryabhata (5th century) on pi and indeterminate equations; Brahmagupta (7th) on
zero and negative quantities; Ramanujan's unproved results and the Hardy
collaboration. Standard history of mathematics.

**When elegance misleads**
String theory's lack of currently testable predictions; supersymmetric particles
not found in expected ranges at the LHC; Hossenfelder's argument that aesthetic
criteria have displaced evidence in theory choice.
- Hossenfelder, S., *Lost in Math* (2018)

**Simplicity as discipline**
Argument, not claim.

**The two cultures, and where India splits them**
C. P. Snow, *The Two Cultures*, Rede Lecture 1959. NEP 2020 removal of rigid
stream separation.

**Occam's razor and its abuse in policy**
Argument, not claim.

**Degrees awarded against work available**
India Skills Report 2025 figures as above.

**The diaspora as distributed advantage**
World Bank: India the largest recipient of remittances in 2024 at about USD 129
billion, up from about 125 billion in 2023; Mexico about 68 billion, the
Philippines about 40 billion, Pakistan about 33 billion. The commonly quoted
diaspora headcount was not confirmed and is not used.
- World Bank Migration and Development Brief, December 2024

**Research spending and the gap it measures**
India's gross expenditure on R&D about 0.64% of GDP (Economic Survey 2025-26);
Israel about 6.3%, South Korea about 5%, United States about 3.4%, China about
2.4% having risen from roughly 0.6% in the late 1990s.
- Economic Survey 2025-26
- Department of Science and Technology, R&D Statistics at a Glance
- Visual Capitalist, *Ranked: The Countries Investing the Most in R&D*

**Semiconductors as strategic doctrine**
India Semiconductor Mission approved 15 December 2021 with an outlay of Rs
76,000 crore; Micron ATMP at Sanand cleared June 2023, first to break ground;
Tata–Powerchip fab at Dholera approved February 2024, 50,000 wafer starts a
month at 28 nm.
- India Semiconductor Mission, ism.gov.in
- PIB releases on Micron Sanand and the Tata–PSMC approval

**Soft power that is cultural rather than technical**
ITEC programme running since 1964; International Day of Yoga observed since 2015.
- Ministry of External Affairs, ITEC programme

**Bills passed without being examined**
PRS: 17th Lok Sabha referred about 16% of bills to committees; 16th about 25%;
14th about 60% and 15th about 71%.
- PRS Legislative Research, "In a rush to pass bills, 17th Lok Sabha is not scrutinising future laws enough"

**Algorithmic sorting against the ideal speech situation**
Habermas on the ideal speech situation; engagement optimisation. Argument, not
statistic.

**Consultation that was promised and rarely happened**
Pre-Legislative Consultation Policy adopted by the Committee of Secretaries on
10 January 2014; minimum thirty days in the public domain. June 2014 to May
2019: 186 bills introduced, 44 published, 24 of those short of thirty days.
About 227 of 301 bills introduced without prior consultation. Government has
stated the policy was never evaluated and no compliance record is kept; it is
not binding.
- Pre-legislative Consultation Policy, 2014 (text)
- LiveLaw, "Participation Without Accountability"
- Centre for Law and Policy Research, "India needs an institutional framework for Pre-legislative Consultations"

**Litigation as institutionalised counter-argument**
Relaxation of locus standi in PIL; standard criticisms. Argument, not statistic.

**The dissent that became the law**
ADM Jabalpur v Shivkant Shukla (1976); Khanna's dissent; superseded for CJI;
44th Amendment 1978 making Article 21 non-suspendable; majority expressly
overruled in Puttaswamy (2017).
- *ADM Jabalpur v Shivkant Shukla*, AIR 1976 SC 1207
- *K. S. Puttaswamy v Union of India* (2017) 10 SCC 1

## Theme 3 — Character, Adversity and the Test of Power

**Post-traumatic growth and what the evidence supports**
Tedeschi and Calhoun's construct; reliance on retrospective self-report;
recovery to baseline more common than growth. Stated as a qualified claim.
- Tedeschi, R. and Calhoun, L., posttraumatic growth literature

**One attempt at everything**
NCRB and Kota figures as in Theme 2; Rajasthan Coaching Centres Act 2025.

**Whether a society lets a founder fail and return**
IBBI: recovery about 49% of claims where resolution completes within the 330-day
limit, falling to about 26% beyond 600 days; average resolution time stretched
well past the statutory limit; recovery across resolved cases about a third of
admitted claims.
- Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India, quarterly newsletters
- NITI Aayog, *Is IBC 2016 Effective?*

**Odisha, and what rebuilding stronger looks like**
1999 super cyclone, winds near 220 km/h, about ten thousand deaths; OSDMA the
first state disaster management authority in India; Cyclone Phailin 2013, over a
million evacuated, 23 deaths; Hudhud, Titli and Fani with double-digit tolls.
- World Bank, *India Averts Cyclone Phailin Devastation*
- UNESCAP, "From storm to strength: Odisha's zero casualty model"

**Deprivation as damage, not curriculum**
Mullainathan and Shafir on the cognitive load of scarcity.
- Mullainathan, S. and Shafir, E., *Scarcity* (2013)

**Risk capital and whether attitudes are shifting**
Argument, not statistic. No failure-rate figure is quoted because the commonly
circulated ones do not trace to a consistent source.

**The career as harbour**, **The straight line and the cost of leaving it**
Argument, not claim.

**Permission to fail safely**
RBI regulatory sandbox framework 2019; first cohort on retail payments opened
November 2019, six entities completed testing and exited; a later cohort on
cross-border remittances.
- Reserve Bank of India, Enabling Framework for Regulatory Sandbox
- Business Standard, "6 entities complete test phase in first cohort under regulatory sandbox"

**Recklessness with what belongs to others**
Argument, with the 2008 crisis as illustration.

**Integrity machinery and whether it functions**
CVC given statutory status in 2003; Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act 2013 following the
2011 anti-corruption movement.
- Central Vigilance Commission Act 2003
- Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act 2013

**A protection that was legislated and never commenced**
Satyendra Dubey, NHAI engineer, murdered 2003 after his complaint was circulated
with his name. Whistle Blowers Protection Act 2014 never brought into force;
Amendment Bill introduced May 2015, passed the Lok Sabha, lapsed on dissolution
of the 16th Lok Sabha.
- PIB, Parliament question on delay in operationalising the Whistleblower Protection Act 2014
- PRS, The Whistle Blowers Protection (Amendment) Bill 2015

**When the auditors and the board saw it early**
Satyam Computer Services, 2009 admission of falsified accounts; IL&FS default
2018 with high ratings until shortly before failure. Structural point about who
pays the assessor.

**Recommendations that were made and not taken**
Second ARC fourth report, *Ethics in Governance*, January 2007; Lokpal Act 2013
drew on its blueprint; Whistle Blowers Protection Act 2014 echoed it.
- Second Administrative Reforms Commission, Fourth Report (DARPG)

**Shocks no internal discipline prevents**
Argument, using the Odisha contrast above.

**Checks that hold and checks that bend**
CAG as a constitutional office; Public Accounts Committee chaired by convention
from the opposition; the timing problem in judicial review. Argument.

**The famous experiments, and the replication fight**
Burger's partial replication (2009), seventy participants to the 150-volt point,
obedience slightly lower than Milgram forty-five years earlier. Stanford prison
experiment: guards coached rather than left to improvise; demand-characteristics
studies; Banuazizi and Movahedi's prediction critique; 2002 BBC prison study
where guards were reluctant to assert authority.
- Burger, J., "Replicating Milgram: Would people still obey today?", *American Psychologist* (2009)
- Le Texier, T., "Debunking the Stanford Prison Experiment", *American Psychologist* (2019)
- Reicher, S. and Haslam, S., BBC Prison Study (2002)

**Power that need not explain itself**
Electoral bonds struck down 15 February 2024 by a unanimous five-judge
Constitution Bench; Article 19(1)(a); sale stopped; SBI directed to disclose
purchases from 12 April 2019; ECI to publish.
- *Association for Democratic Reforms v Union of India* (2024)
- Supreme Court Observer, Constitutionality of the Electoral Bond Scheme

**Where accountability is thinnest**
Argument, not claim.

**Leaders who handed power back**
Cincinnatus (traditional account); Washington declining a third term; Mandela
serving one term and standing down in 1999.

**Horizons longer than a term**, **What short tenure costs**,
**The arithmetic of patience**, **When delay is itself the harm**,
**Instruments for seeing what a day hides**
Arguments rather than sourced claims, except the general reference to birth
cohort studies in the last, which rests on the standard longitudinal literature.
