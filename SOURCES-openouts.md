# Sources for "Open it out with" material

Every factual claim in `openouts.js` was checked against a source before it was
written. This file records what was checked and where, so any figure in the
material can be traced. Entries are listed by theme and sub-theme.

A claim that could not be confirmed was left out rather than softened. Two
examples from Theme 1: the exact date of India's last smallpox case is reported
inconsistently across sources, so the material says "May 1975"; and the FRBM
Act's deficit target was dropped from the preparedness entry because the
searched sources did not tie it to disaster financing.

---

## Theme 1 — Ends, Means and the Ethics of Decision

### 1.1 Do the means contaminate the end?

**No-first-use and the threat never carried out**
Doctrine adopted by the Cabinet Committee on Security, January 2003; credible
minimum deterrence; retaliation only against a nuclear attack on Indian
territory or forces; retaliation "massive" and designed to inflict unacceptable
damage; reserved nuclear response to major chemical or biological attack.
- Arms Control Association, *Twenty-Five Years of Overt Nuclear India*
- Observer Research Foundation, *Beyond Current Nuclear Doctrine Debates*

**Force as signal rather than as objective**
Uri attack 18 September 2016, 19 soldiers killed; strikes on launch pads across
the LoC 29 September 2016. Pulwama bombing 14 February 2019, 40 CRPF personnel
killed; Balakot strike 26 February 2019; Foreign Secretary's description of it
as a non-military pre-emptive action.
- *2019 Balakot airstrike*, Wikipedia
- Stimson Center, *Three Years After Balakot*
- The Round Table, "India's Pakistan policy: from 2016 'surgical strike' to 2019 Balakot 'airstrike'"

**Coercion without combat**
UN Security Council Resolution 661, August 1990, comprehensive sanctions on
Iraq; oil-for-food programme created December 1996; criticism of delays,
reparation deductions and politically motivated holds; later shift to targeted
sanctions.
- Congressional Research Service, *Iraq: Humanitarian Needs, Impact of Sanctions, and the "Oil for Food" Program*
- MERIP, *The Enduring Lessons of the Iraq Sanctions*
- Global Policy Forum, *Iraq Sanctions: Humanitarian Implications and Options for the Future*

**A lawful end pursued unlawfully**
PUCL v State of Maharashtra (2014) 10 SCC 635: 99 encounters, 135 killed by
Mumbai police 1995–1997; sixteen guidelines; force of law under Article 141;
independent investigation required. National Security Act 1980: three months
before Advisory Board reference, Board of three High Court judges, twelve month
maximum, no legal representation before the Board.
- *PUCL v State of Maharashtra*, Indian Kanoon
- Bar and Bench, "Even State has no authority to violate Article 21"
- Ministry of Home Affairs, text of the National Security Act 1980

**When the outcome may genuinely outweigh the route**
Bihar and Uttar Pradesh about three-quarters of global smallpox cases in 1974;
Operation Smallpox Zero 1975; documented intimidation and coercion in the final
stages; last indigenous Indian case May 1975; global eradication declared 1980.
- Greenough, P., "Intimidation, coercion and resistance in the final stages of the South Asian Smallpox Eradication Campaign, 1973–1975", PubMed
- *1974 smallpox epidemic in India*, Wikipedia
- University of Michigan online exhibit, *Zero Smallpox*

### 1.3 Intuition, evidence and the limits of calculation

**What a trial establishes and what it cannot**
2019 Nobel to Banerjee, Duflo and Kremer; Kremer's Kenya schooling trials from
the mid-1990s; internal validity from randomisation; Deaton and Cartwright on
external validity.
- Nobel Prize press release, Economic Sciences 2019
- Deaton and Cartwright, NBER Working Paper 22595
- CEPR, *What randomisation can and cannot do*

**When the measure becomes the target**
Goodhart's law; Campbell's law; SDG India Index across 113 indicators aligned to
the National Indicator Framework; Aspirational Districts Programme launched
January 2018 covering 112 districts with delta rankings.
- *Goodhart's law*, Wikipedia
- NITI Aayog, SDG India Index 2023-24 (PIB release)

**A model that travels, and what it needs to survive the journey**
MGR launched the Tamil Nadu noon meal scheme 1 July 1982; national programme
1995; Supreme Court order of 28 November 2001 requiring 300 calories and 8–12g
protein for 200 days. Gandaman, Saran district, 16 July 2013: 23 children died,
monocrotophos, oil taken from a pesticide container.
- Ramanathapuram District administration, Puratchi Thalaivar MGR Noon Meal Programme
- *Bihar school meal poisoning incident*, Wikipedia
- Down To Earth, "A year on, no lessons learnt from Bihar mid day meal tragedy"

**When the default is denial**
Drèze and colleagues: about 1,000 households across 32 villages in Jharkhand,
exclusion up to twenty per cent where biometric authentication was required for
every sale. Right to Food Campaign: at least 57 hunger-related deaths 2015–2018,
at least 19 linked to Aadhaar-related exclusion.
- Drèze et al., *Aadhaar and Food Security in Jharkhand: Pain without Gain?*
- The Wire, "Aadhaar-Based PDS Means Denial of Rations For Many"

**What the field knows that the file does not**
Shift from mass vaccination to search and containment; ring vaccination around
each case; Bihar and UP as the centre of the endgame.
- University of Michigan online exhibit, *Chronology of the eradication campaign*
- Vaccine, "Lessons from smallpox eradication campaign in Bihar state and in India"

### 1.4 When custom is not a guide

**The courts between custom and constitutional right**
*Shayara Bano v Union of India*, 22 August 2017, five-judge bench, 3:2, talaq-e-
biddat struck down; Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Marriage) Act 2019.
*Indian Young Lawyers Association v State of Kerala*, 28 September 2018, 4:1,
Justice Indu Malhotra dissenting; more than fifty review petitions.
- *Shayara Bano v Union of India*, (2017) 9 SCC 1
- *Indian Young Lawyers Association v The State of Kerala*, Indian Kanoon
- India Code, Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Marriage) Act 2019

**Who decides what is essential**
Shirur Mutt (1954), seven-judge bench, essentiality determined by reference to
the doctrines of the religion itself. November 2019 reference; nine-judge bench
hearings began 7 April 2026; judgment reserved 14 May 2026; 66 tagged matters.
- Supreme Court Observer, *Sabarimala Review* case page
- The Leaflet, "Sabarimala Reference | Nine-judge Constitution Bench reserves judgment"

**Sanction that operates below the law**
*Shakti Vahini v Union of India* (2018) 7 SCC 192; Articles 19 and 21; special
cells, safe houses, round-the-clock helpline.
- *Shakti Vahini v Union of India*, AIR 2018 SC 1601

**Burke's caution about dismantling faster than you can replace**
*Reflections on the Revolution in France*, 1790.

**Vidyasagar, Roy, and the limits of arguing from inside**
Bengal Sati Regulation issued by Lord William Bentinck, 4 December 1829.
Vidyasagar, principal of Sanskrit College, published his case in 1855 arguing
from the Parashara Smriti; Hindu Widows' Remarriage Act 1856.
- *Hindu Widows' Remarriage Act, 1856*, Wikipedia
- NextIAS, *Hindu Widow Remarriage Act, 1856*

### 1.5 The ideal that does not arrive but still directs

**Ideals that cannot be enforced but still legislate**
Article 37; Article 45 to Article 21A via the 86th Amendment 2002; RTE Act 2009;
Article 39A and the Legal Services Authorities Act 1987; Article 41 and MGNREGA
2005.
- Constitution of India, Part IV
- Unacademy, *86th Amendment 2002*

**The annual audit an essay can perform**
Method rather than claim. Preamble text only.

**The honest arithmetic of the distance**
SDG India Index composite 57 (2018), 66 (2020-21), 71 (2023-24); 113 indicators;
state range 57–79 against 42–69 in 2018; Uttarakhand and Kerala at 79; SDG-13
from 54 to 67.
- PIB, *Release of SDG India Index 2023-24*
- UN India, "India making progress towards SDGs, report shows"

**Repairing the roof in good weather**
Disaster Management Act 2005, in force January 2006; three-tier structure; NDMA
chaired by the Prime Minister; National Disaster Management Plan 2016 first
aligned to the Sendai Framework; Sendai's four priorities for action.
- NDMA, National Disaster Management Plan 2016
- UNDRR, Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction 2015–2030

**Unreachable ideals as an excuse for permanent underperformance**
Constituent Assembly criticism of the Directive Principles as an unenforceable
promise; Article 45's eventual conversion into Article 21A.
- Constituent Assembly Debates, Part IV
